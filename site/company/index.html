What is free and what is paid

The recorder, the GitHub Action and the docs are open source under Apache 2.0 and always will be. You can run all of it on your own machines and in your own CI without an account.

The hosted service is the paid part: rendering with no CI setup, stable video URLs, take history and pull request previews. Payments are taken by Stripe on behalf of ISHE CO HOLDINGS LIMITED, which is the merchant of record and appears on your statement and invoices.

Trademark
